## 📱 **YOUR HEALTH & FITNESS APP FEATURES:**

### **Navigation Sidebar** (White panel on left):
- 📊 **Dashboard** - View daily & weekly health metrics
- 📱 **Devices** - Manage wearable devices
- 👥 **Sharing** - Share data with healthcare professionals
- 🌍 **Language Toggle** - Switch between Dutch/English

### **Dashboard View:**
Shows "Today" section with 4 metric cards:
- **Steps** - Daily step count with progress bar
- **Calories** - Calorie burn with goal tracking
- **Heart Rate** - Current/Max/Min BPM
- **Distance** - Kilometers traveled

Plus "This Week" summary showing:
- Total steps, distance, calories
- Average heart rate
- Active days

### **Devices View:**
- Scan for wearable devices
- View connected devices
- Sync data from wearables
- Connect/Disconnect functionality
- Battery status display

### **Sharing View:**
- Add recipients (Doctor, Physiotherapist, Personal Trainer)
- View shared access list
- Revoke access anytime
- GDPR compliance warnings
- Email and permissions management
